PLAYER REPORTS

Report a player

Saw someone cheating, scamming or breaking the rules? Tell us what happened and attach your evidence. Staff review every report and record their decision.

How reports work

  1. SubmitPick the gamemode and category, explain what happened and add evidence.
  2. Staff reviewA staff member opens your report and marks it under review.
  3. DecisionIt is approved or denied with a written reason from the staff member who handled it.
Good evidence
  • A clear screenshot or a short clip showing the rule break
  • The approximate time and the gamemode
  • Faction names or coordinates when relevant

Wrongly banned? Use Ban Appeals instead. Need urgent help? Open Discord.

Six steps to faction dominance

Never played Minecraft Factions before? Don't worry! Here's a quick beginner's guide to how CrownPVP – Semi OP Factions works.

  1. 🏰 Create or join a faction

    • Create your own faction or join a team.
    • Invite friends and work together.
    • Build alliances and compete against enemies.

  2. 🏗️ Build your base

    • Claim land for your faction.
    • Build hidden or fortified bases.
    • Protect your valuables from enemy raids.

  3. 🌾 Farm and make money

    • Create farms and mob grinders.
    • Collect and sell resources.
    • Earn money for spawners, gear and upgrades.

  4. ⚔️ Get stronger

    • Collect custom weapons and armor sets.
    • Use powerful custom enchantments.
    • Fight other players and collect their loot.

  5. 💣 Raid enemy bases

    • Build TNT cannons to attack enemy bases.
    • Break through their defenses.
    • Steal their items and resources, and defend your own base.

  6. 🏆 Compete for cash prizes

    • Increase your faction's total value.
    • Collect valuable spawners and resources.
    • Climb the /f top leaderboard and win real cash prizes!

Faction Raid Shield System

New

To keep raids fair and competitive, and to prevent shield abuse, OP Factions uses a 5-hour raid window.

5h open19h closed

🟢 5 hours – shield open

  • Factions are vulnerable to raids.
  • Enemies can attack, TNT and cannon your base.
  • Defend your territory and protect your loot!

🔴 19 hours – shield closed

  • When the 5-hour window ends, shields activate automatically.
  • Your faction is protected from raids.
  • No raiding until the next raid window.

🔄 Repeating cycle

5 hours open, then 19 hours closed. It repeats every 24 hours.

⚠️ Why are we doing this?

It prevents shield abuse, stops factions from manipulating protection times, and gives everyone a fair chance to raid and defend.

Prepare your cannons. Protect your base. Choose your raid time wisely. No permanent safety. No unfair advantages. Just pure Factions war!

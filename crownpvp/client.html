THE ESSENTIALS

Useful information.

Planned features are focused on visibility and customization. This page describes the direction, not a released product.

01 / PERFORMANCE

Performance-focused setup

Optimization options designed to keep the interface responsive. Actual FPS will always depend on your hardware and settings.

02 / PLAYER HUD

Customizable PvP HUD

Choose which information appears on screen and arrange a layout that suits your play style.

03 / LIVE INPUTS

FPS, CPS, ping & keystrokes

Keep performance and input information visible without crowding the gameplay view.

04 / PLAYER STATUS

Armor & potion status

Planned durability and effect indicators give you a quick read during a fight.

05 / BUILD TOOLS

Schematic support

Planned schematic viewing for building references. Use it only where the server you play on allows it.

06 / PERSONALIZATION

Clean visual controls

Configure HUD positions and visual options without adding unnecessary on-screen elements.

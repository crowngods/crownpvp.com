Real-time glass, in five terms.

Iridescence — Angle-dependent color that blooms across a surface as it catches the light.

Index of refraction — The ratio that describes how much light bends as it enters a material.

Dispersion — The separation of white light into its component colors as it passes through glass.

Transmission — The light that travels through a surface, carrying the scene beyond it.

Roughness — The microtexture that turns a sharp reflection into a soft studio gradient.

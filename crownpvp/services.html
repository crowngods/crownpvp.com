      FAQ
      Good to know.
      How long does a project take?
      Timing depends on scope, content and integrations. We confirm a realistic delivery schedule before work begins.
      When do I pay?
      Payment terms are agreed after the scope and price are confirmed in your project request. Nothing is charged on this page.
      Can I request changes?
      Yes. The included revision rounds and review process are confirmed with the package or project scope.
      Can you work with my existing branding?
      Absolutely. Share your logo, colors and references, or we can propose a visual direction that fits your project.
      What Minecraft versions do you support?
      PvP resource packs are designed for Minecraft Java versions 1.8 through 26.3. Plugin compatibility is confirmed against your server version and setup.

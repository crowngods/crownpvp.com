BAN APPEALS

Appeal a punishment

Think your ban or mute should be reconsidered? Send an appeal with your side of the story. A staff member reviews it and explains their decision.

Before you appeal

  1. Find your punishmentSearch your username on the Punishments page and click your punishment to see its ID.
  2. Send one appealBe honest and specific. You can have one open appeal at a time.
  3. Follow the decisionYou get an alert on the bell and can read the decision and reason under My appeals.
Please do not
  • Open several appeals for the same punishment
  • Insult or pressure staff
  • Share fake evidence. It leads to a longer punishment

Want to report another player instead? Use Reports.
